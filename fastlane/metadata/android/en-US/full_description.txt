SecureTap sends a password, link, message or small photo from one Android phone to another when you tap them together. Everything is encrypted end to end with a fresh key for each transfer, and nothing goes through the internet.

Put one phone on Send and the other on Receive, then hold them back to back.

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>Send text, passwords, links and photos</li>
<li>Encrypted with ECDH P-256 and AES-256-GCM, with a new key for every transfer</li>
<li>A verification code under Details, to check that nobody intercepted the transfer</li>
<li>Send the same thing to several phones one at a time, then it's wiped when you tap Done, or after 2 minutes</li>
<li>Photos are shrunk and stripped of location and other metadata before sending</li>
<li>Copied passwords are hidden from the clipboard preview, and screenshots are blocked</li>
<li>If the phones slip apart, tap again to pick up where it left off</li>
</ul>

NFC is slow, so a photo means holding the phones together for 10 to 20 seconds. Sending phone to phone works on most phones, but a few NFC chips don't handle it well.

Requires Android 10 or later with NFC.
